There are a few things that help describe who I am. They may sound like simple traits, but at the core, they make up the foundation of my personality — so you know exactly what kind of person I am when I stand in front of you.

Competition

All sports have a winner. In business, you compete with the other firms in your industry — and while there may be no "losers," the "winners" often enjoy higher market share, higher profits, a higher chance of survival. On an individual level I strive to compete in much of what I do, whether against myself and my personal records or against my peers, in the workplace or on a sports field.

Teamwork

This may seem like a contradiction, but there is no satisfaction higher than winning as a team. When my soccer team won a tournament, the sheer joy of celebrating our hard-earned victory together — very few moments come close. When a colleague struggles and I struggle alongside them, and together we make it through, the relief and celebration is much sweeter shared than taken alone.

Relationships

To some, networking has become a dirty word. To me it has always meant the same thing: making friends and helping each other out. It's that split second of happiness when you run into an old friend in a place you least expected — the feeling of walking into a familiar room and knowing you aren't alone.

"Wherever I go, I want to be remembered — not for showing up or doing the minimum, but for being the best, for caring and helping others, and most importantly, for having a positive impact on those who choose to be around me."
